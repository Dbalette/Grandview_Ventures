Chicago Nights plans a night out in Chicago - pick a vibe, a budget, and a night of the week, and it builds a real route between real bars, lounges, and late-night spots, with directions for each stop.

Where does the venue data come from?
A curated list of real Chicago venues researched from local guides, refreshed periodically. If you add your own Google Places API key, the app can also look up additional venues and refresh hours automatically - this is entirely optional.

Does this app require an internet connection?
No, not for planning a night - the core venue list and itinerary planner work fully offline. An internet connection is only used when you tap "Directions" (to open Apple Maps) or if you've configured an optional Google Places API key for live venue lookups.

Does the app use my location?
No. Chicago Nights does not request or use your location. Directions open in Apple Maps, which handles your location itself, separately from this app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Chicago Nights does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system and does not request your location.

What data is stored: Your preferences (such as your chosen vibe, budget, or neighborhood) and a cache of venue data are kept locally on your device. This data never leaves your device - it is not transmitted to us, to Apple, or to any third party.

What data we fetch: Tapping "Directions" opens Apple Maps directly with the venue's name and neighborhood - this app does not access or store your location as part of that. If you've added your own Google Places API key, the app makes outbound requests to Google's Places API to look up public business information (venue names, categories, hours). These are one-way reads of public data - no personal or device information is sent as part of these requests beyond what's inherent to any standard network request.

What data we collect: None. Chicago Nights uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and has no account system.

Third parties: We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. If you configure your own Google Places API key, your requests to Google are subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy: Because no personal data is collected from any user, this app does not collect personal information from children.
