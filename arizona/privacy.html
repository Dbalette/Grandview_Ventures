Arizona Water Watch

Support

Arizona Water Watch tracks Arizona's water story with real data on reservoirs, groundwater, drought, and rivers - explained in plain English, with sources you can check.

Where does the data come from?
Live public data from official government sources covering water, drought, and hydrology reporting across Arizona. Each figure in the app is labeled as provisional, reported, modeled, reviewed, or sample data, so you always know how solid a number is.

Does this app require an internet connection?
Yes. Arizona Water Watch fetches live readings from public government data services to stay current.

Does the app use my location?
No. Arizona Water Watch does not request or use your location - it presents statewide and site-level data for fixed monitoring locations across Arizona.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Arizona Water Watch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system and does not request your location.

What data is stored: Any preferences (such as saved views) are kept locally on your device. This data never leaves your device - it is not transmitted to us, to Apple, or to any third party.

What data we fetch: The app makes outbound requests to public government water-data services to display current conditions. These are one-way reads of public data - no personal or device information is sent as part of these requests beyond what's inherent to any standard network request.

What data we collect: None. Arizona Water Watch uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and has no account system.

Third parties: We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy: Because no personal data is collected from any user, this app does not collect personal information from children.
