What the mirror is

Mirror Mirror on The Wall is an entertainment app. It measures proportions on a picture of your face and compares them with the golden ratio and a few classical and modern rules of proportion. The score it gives is arithmetic, not an assessment of your worth, your health or your attractiveness to anyone. The golden ratio is a historical hypothesis about beauty that controlled studies do not support well, and the app says so on its methodology page. Please take the mirror in the spirit of a parlour game.
