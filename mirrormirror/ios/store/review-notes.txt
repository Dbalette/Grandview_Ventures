No accounts or sign-in. No test credentials are needed.

Everything runs on the device: the front camera feeds an on-device face landmark model, the proportions are computed locally, and nothing is uploaded. The app works offline.

Three readings are free. Opening the mirror a fourth time shows the paywall for Mirror Mirror Premium, an auto-renewing subscription of $0.99 per month. Restore Purchases is on the paywall. To see the paywall quickly, take three readings; the photo option ("Use a photo instead" on the first screen) is the fastest way to do that without the camera.

Camera permission is requested when you tap "Open the mirror". The reading can also be taken from a photograph, with no camera permission.

The score is entertainment: the app compares proportions with the golden ratio and states plainly that the golden ratio is not a validated measure of attractiveness. It makes no medical, health or psychological claims.
