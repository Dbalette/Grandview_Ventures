Colorado River Upper Basin Watch

Support

Colorado River Upper Basin Watch tracks the water story of the Colorado River's Upper Basin, the four states of Colorado, New Mexico, Utah, and Wyoming, with real data on reservoirs, rivers, snowpack, and drought, explained in plain English, with sources you can check.

Where does the data come from?
Live public data from official government sources covering reservoir and river levels, mountain snowpack, and drought across the basin. Each figure in the app is labeled by how solid it is: provisional, reviewed, reported, last-known, or unavailable.

Does this app require an internet connection?
Yes. Colorado River Upper Basin Watch fetches live readings from public government water-data services to stay current.

Does the app use my location?
No. Colorado River Upper Basin Watch does not request or use your location. It presents basin-wide and site-level data for fixed monitoring locations across the Upper Basin.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Colorado River Upper Basin Watch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and does not request your location.

What data is stored: Any preferences (such as saved locations) are kept locally on your device. This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, to Apple, or to any third party.

What data we fetch: The app makes outbound requests to public government water-data services to display current conditions. These are one-way reads of public data. No personal or device information is sent as part of these requests beyond what is inherent to any standard network request. As with any request to any internet server, the operators of those services necessarily receive the network address each request came from, and their handling of it is governed by their own policies. We receive none of it.

What data we collect: None. Colorado River Upper Basin Watch uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and has no account system. Nothing you do in this app is reported to us.

Third parties: The only third parties involved are the public government data services the app reads from, as described above. We share no data with anyone, because we collect none.

Data retention and deletion: We hold nothing, so there is nothing to request the deletion of. Everything the app stores stays on your device: your preferences, such as saved locations. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device permanently. Because there is no account and nothing is sent to us, there is no consent to withdraw and no data for us to return or erase on your behalf. If you have a question about any of this, write to the address below.

Children's privacy: Because no personal data is collected from any user, this app does not collect personal information from children.
