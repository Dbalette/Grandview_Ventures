Upper Basin Water Watch

Support

Upper Basin Water Watch tracks the four states above the dividing point, with the shared reservoir bank, the delivery duty and each state's own readings in one place: real readings on reservoirs, rivers, groundwater, drought and snow, explained in plain English.

Where does the data come from?
Live public data from official government sources covering water, drought, and hydrology reporting across the basin. Each figure in the app is labeled as provisional, reported, modeled, reviewed, or sample data, so you always know how solid a number is.

Does this app require an internet connection?
Yes. Upper Basin Water Watch fetches live readings from public government data services to stay current.

Does the app use my location?
No. Upper Basin Water Watch does not request or use your location - it presents statewide and site-level data for fixed monitoring locations across the basin.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Upper Basin Water Watch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system. It asks for your location only when you tap the control that checks whether an alert covers you, and that comparison happens on the device.

What data is stored: Any preferences (such as saved views) are kept locally on your device. This data never leaves your device - it is not transmitted to us, to Apple, or to any third party.

What data we fetch: The app makes outbound requests to public government water-data services to display current conditions. These are one-way reads of public data - no personal or device information is sent as part of these requests beyond what's inherent to any standard network request.

What data we collect: None. Upper Basin Water Watch uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and has no account system.

Third parties: We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy: Because no personal data is collected from any user, this app does not collect personal information from children.
