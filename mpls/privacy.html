MPLS Crime Watch - Privacy Policy

MPLS Crime Watch does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Location

The app uses your device's location solely to show incidents near you, compute nearby alerts, and assess area risk. Your location is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Data sources

The app displays publicly available public-safety data. Requests to the public data service contain no personal information about you.

Notifications

Alerts are generated locally on your device.
