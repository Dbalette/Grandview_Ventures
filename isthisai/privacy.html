Is This AI?

Support

Is This AI? gives you the chances that an image was generated by artificial intelligence. Point your camera at a picture on another screen or in print, or pick a screenshot you already have, and the app estimates how likely it is to have been made by AI.

Everything is analyzed on your iPhone. Your images are never uploaded. The app makes no network requests at all, so it works in airplane mode.

How accurate is it?
It is an estimate, not a verdict. The app shows the uncertainty alongside every percentage and tells you when an image is the kind it struggles with. It cannot prove who made an image or that an image is authentic.

Why did it get one wrong?
Detectors are least reliable on images that are both small and already heavily compressed, which is common for pictures that have been shared and re-shared. If you can find the original file, analyzing that gives a much better answer. Images made by generators newer than the ones the model learned from are also harder to catch.

Can it tell if a real photo was edited with AI?
Not reliably, and the app says so on the result screen rather than implying otherwise. It estimates whether an image was generated outright.

Does it need an internet connection?
No. The detection model ships inside the app. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is uploaded.

Does it use my location?
No. The app never requests location.

Why does it ask for the camera?
Only when you tap Take Photo, so you can photograph an image on another screen or in print. Choosing an existing image uses the system photo picker, which does not require access to your photo library at all.

What does it cost?
Ninety nine cents, one time. There is no subscription, no account, no advertising, and no recurring charge.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Is This AI? does not collect, transmit, or share any data. There is no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

Your images: Images you capture or select are analyzed entirely on your device by a model bundled inside the app. They are held in memory only while you are looking at the result and are discarded when you analyze another image or leave the screen. The app never writes them to storage, never uploads them, and never shares them with us, with Apple, or with anyone else.

Network use: None. The app makes no outbound network requests during normal operation. No image, no hash of an image, no metadata, no detection result, and no analytics payload is ever sent anywhere. The app functions identically with no internet connection.

What data we collect: None at all. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting service.

Stored on your device: Only small interface preferences, kept locally. This never leaves your device.

Permissions: The camera permission is requested only when you tap Take Photo. Selecting an existing image uses Apple's system photo picker, which runs outside the app and gives it access only to the single image you choose, so no photo library permission is requested.

Children: The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes: If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
