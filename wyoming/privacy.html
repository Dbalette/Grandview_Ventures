Wyoming Water Watch

Support

Wyoming Water Watch tracks Wyoming's water story with real data on reservoirs, rivers, snowpack, drought, and groundwater, plus water news and history, explained in plain English, with sources you can check.

Where does the data come from?
Live public data from official government sources covering reservoir and river levels, mountain snowpack, statewide drought, drinking-water violation history, and active flood alerts. Each figure in the app is labeled by how solid it is: live, last known, or unavailable. News headlines come from public news search feeds. Sentiment on each headline is scored entirely on your device and never sent anywhere.

Does this app require an internet connection?
Yes. Wyoming Water Watch fetches live readings from public government water-data services and public news feeds to stay current.

Does the app use my location?
Only for one purpose. If you open the app while an official flood alert is active, it can check whether that alert's warned area covers where you are, so that alert can be shown first. This check happens entirely on your device. Your location is never stored, never transmitted anywhere, and never used for anything else. You can decline the permission prompt and the app works the same, just without that one alert-ordering convenience.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Wyoming Water Watch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising. If you choose to share your location, it is used only on your device, as described below.

What data is stored: Your preferences, such as saved news stories or wells, and any readings the app has kept from an earlier fetch so it can show a last-known value when a source is unreachable, are kept locally on your device. None of it is transmitted to us, to Apple, or to any third party.

What the app fetches: The app makes outbound requests to public government water-data services and public news search feeds to display current conditions and headlines. These are one-way reads of public data. No personal or device information is sent as part of these requests beyond what is inherent to any standard network request. As with any request to any internet server, the operators of those services necessarily receive the network address each request came from, and their handling of it is governed by their own policies. We receive none of it. The home screen's map is drawn by Apple Maps, so Apple's map servers receive the same kind of request when the map loads, under Apple's own privacy policy. Your location is not part of those requests.

Location: Optional, and used for one purpose only. If an official flood alert is active when you open the app, it can check on your device whether that alert's warned area covers where you are, so that alert can be shown first. Only the yes-or-no result of that check reaches the screen. Your location is never stored, never transmitted anywhere, and never used for anything else. You can decline the permission prompt, or withdraw the permission at any time in your device's Settings, and the app works the same without it, apart from that one alert-ordering convenience.

On-device processing: News sentiment scores are computed entirely on your device. Headline text is never sent anywhere for this analysis.

What data we collect: None. Wyoming Water Watch uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and has no account system. Nothing you do in this app is reported to us.

Third parties: The only third parties involved are the public government data services and public news search feeds the app reads from, as described above, and Apple Maps, which draws the home-screen map. We share no data with anyone, because we collect none.

Data retention and deletion: We hold nothing, so there is nothing to request the deletion of. Everything the app stores stays on your device, including your preferences, saved news stories, saved wells, and any readings kept from an earlier fetch. Your location, when you share it, is used in the moment and never stored. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device permanently. The only permission the app asks for is location, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device's Settings. Beyond that, because there is no account and nothing is sent to us, there is no data for us to return or erase on your behalf. If you have a question about any of this, write to the address below.

Children's privacy: Because no personal data is collected from any user, this app does not collect personal information from children.
