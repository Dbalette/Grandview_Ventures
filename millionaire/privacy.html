Millionaire Trader

Support

Millionaire Trader is a stock market simulation game for Mac. Start with $10,000, trade stocks and options across weekly sessions, and try to reach a million dollars.

How do I save my progress?
Your game saves automatically to your player profile after each action. You can maintain multiple player profiles from the title screen.

Does this app require an internet connection?
No. Millionaire Trader runs entirely offline — all game data stays on your Mac.

How do I remove a saved player?
Use "Clear Player Directory" on the title screen to remove all saved profiles.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Millionaire Trader does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline.

What data is stored: Your game progress (player initials, cash, portfolio, and score history) is saved locally on your Mac, inside the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device — it is not transmitted to us, to Apple, or to any third party.

What data we collect: None. Millionaire Trader makes no network requests, uses no analytics or advertising SDKs, and has no account system.

Third parties: We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy: Because no data is collected from any user, this app does not collect personal information from children.
